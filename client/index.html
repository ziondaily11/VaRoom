Questions?

Frequently asked.

How does GPS verification work?

When a host lists a space, our system checks their GPS location against the address they provided. This ensures every listing is real and the host can actually access it. No fake locations, no bait-and-switch.

Is my payment safe?

Yes. Payments are held in escrow through our partner until you confirm the space matches the listing photos and description. If something is wrong, you're protected by our full refund guarantee.

Can I message the host before booking?

Absolutely. Every listing shows the host's contact info and you can message directly through VaRoom. Ask questions, negotiate rates, or discuss your specific needs before committing.

Do I need an agent?

No. VaRoom connects you directly to hosts. There are no agent fees, no middlemen, and no hidden costs. You and the host negotiate directly on the platform.

How long does it take to find a space?

Most guests find something suitable within 10–20 minutes. Our search filters and Elie AI assistant help you narrow down options by area, budget, and space type. If you need help, you can reach out to hosts directly.
