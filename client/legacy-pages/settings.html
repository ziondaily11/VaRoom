Help

Help CentreAnswers to common questions

How do bookings work? Request a stay, the host approves or declines, then you'll see it in your Bookings tab.

How do I list a space as a host? Switch to hosting from your profile, then use "List a space" on your host dashboard.

Can I pay through VaRoom yet? Not yet — online payments are being rolled out. Until then, coordinate payment with your host directly via chat.
